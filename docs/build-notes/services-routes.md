## 5. Requests to „shared“ (kit / partials — we did not edit them)

- **R1 · HLI CTA overlap (bug, `emul/lists-cards.css`).** `.nectar-hor-list-item:has(> .nectar-list-item-btn){padding-right:64px}` loses to the later `.nectar-hor-list-item[data-hover-effect="default"]{padding-inline:20px}` (same specificity). As a result, long text runs under the → arrow. This shows on the P3 service rows at 1440 and on every Link row at 390 (T2 „…ir atgal;“, GS-Paslaugos). Fix: move the `:has()` rule below, or raise its specificity (`.nectar-hor-list-item[data-hover-effect]:has(> .nectar-list-item-btn)`). Better still, render the CTA as its own last grid column, which is what Salient does.
- **R2 · Color Change Section text (emulation gap, `emul.js` initColorChange + CSS).** Salient 18.2.1 writes `--nectar-page-text-color` and `--nectar-page-light-text-color` on `:root` for the active section. Text in color-change rows follows it, so the chapter that is leaving flips together with the background. The kit only swaps the background colour. As a result, at every T2–T10 boundary one chapter's text sits on its own colour: paper text on asphalt before the switch, snow text on paper after it. You can see this in any screenshot taken between chapters (e.g. T8 → T9, T6 → T7, T9 → T10). Request: when a row becomes active, point the tone variables (`--tone-fg`, `--tone-muted`, `--tone-hair(-2)`, `--tone-label`, `--tone-inv-*`) of **all** `[data-color-change]` rows at the active row's tone, for example with a root attribute `data-cc-tone="light|dark"` and 2 CSS blocks. Rows that have their own background image or overlay (T8) keep their tone. This affects the home page and the service template too.
- **R3 · Sticky Content Sections → Content Alignment (missing option).** 18.2.1 has *Content Alignment* Middle / Stretch / Top / Bottom. The pages already carry `data-content-alignment="stretch"` (P3) and `"middle"` (T4). Expected behaviour: the section becomes a flex column and `.nectar-sticky-media-section__inner` gets `flex:1`. With stretch, the Inner Row fills the section height (Equal Height columns, and a Fit-to-Container image fills the card). With middle, the content is centred vertically. Once this lands, P3 can go back to Section Height 80–85vh.
- **R4 · Tall pinned sections (optional).** A pinned section taller than `100vh − top` never shows its bottom while stuck, because the next section covers it (Salient behaves the same way). A guard would help, e.g. stack the container (`nsms-off`) when a section's content height exceeds the available height. Both pages are designed to fit at ≥ 1024×768 and 1280×720 without it.
- **R5 · `data-salient` on element roots inside partials.** The 15 / 23 unlabelled roots that `check.py` counts on our pages all come from GS-Poraštė (Text Reveal Wave links, responsive text) and GS-Pasitikėjimas (8 Horizontal List Items).
- **R6 · Legacy files, Wave 3 delete.** `assets/css/pages/paslaugos.css`, `assets/css/pages/tarptautiniai.css`, `assets/js/pages/paslaugos.js`, `assets/js/pages/tarptautiniai.js` and `docs/build-notes/pages-services-routes.md` are no longer referenced by our pages.
- **R7 · NEW strings for `turinio-pataisos.md`.** Labels: „Dviem maršrutais“, „Keturios šalys pakeliui“ (H2), „4 šalys“, „Spauskite žymeklį schemoje“, „Kelionė per Europą“, „Atgal iš Airijos:“ / „Atgal iš Ispanijos:“ (the GS-Artimiausi wording), „Pakeliui · kryptis“, the group counters „0N / 06 · N paslaugos“, and the P2 metas „01 · LT ⇄ IE · LT ⇄ ES“, „02 · Artimiausias išvykimas“, „03 · Siuntos kodas“.
